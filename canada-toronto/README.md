# Canada–Toronto: Electoral Decision Twin V19.3

The [locked Deep Zoom interactive experience](v19.3/index.html) was recovered from the saved artifact `Electoral_Decision_Twin_V19_3_Deep_Zoom.html` and transferred without intentional edits.

This branch of the research investigates the differences between what voters tell us, what forecasts estimate, what election results show, and what the evidence can establish about causation. It covers Canadian federal electoral geography and Toronto's municipal wards.

**Important:** This is the standalone HTML, not yet the complete reproducible research package. The full V19.3 archive, validation scripts, source registry, historical polling inputs, geography/data sources, and QA notes are still awaiting recovery and upload. Do not equate an accessible interactive story with independently verified model calibration or a complete validated data archive.

## Version preservation

Keep the V19.3 HTML immutable as an approved release. Any modifications should be staged in a subsequent versioned folder. Preserve the research provenance and distinguish observed data from illustrative examples or inferred model output.
